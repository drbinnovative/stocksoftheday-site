The pick

LifeStance Health Group (LFST) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on November 9, 2026. Volume was 8.6x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 6.03%.

What happened

On November 5, LifeStance reported third-quarter 2026 results. Revenue was $455.9 million, up 25% from $363.8 million a year earlier. Net income was $22.4 million, compared with $1.1 million. Adjusted EBITDA was $68.4 million, compared with $40.2 million.

The company also raised its full-year 2026 outlook. It now expects revenue of $1.741 billion to $1.761 billion and Adjusted EBITDA of $242 million to $250 million. For the fourth quarter, it expects revenue of $446 million to $466 million.

These filings are dated November 5, four days before the volume spike. The company filings we found do not describe a specific event on November 9.

What news reports said

Yahoo Finance published a LifeStance announcement on November 9 saying that certain stockholders, called the Selling Stockholders, intended to offer 45,000,000 shares of LifeStance common stock in a secondary public offering. [Yahoo Finance]

Later that day, Yahoo Finance published a second announcement. It reported that LifeStance announced the pricing of a secondary underwritten public offering of 45,000,000 shares at a public offering price of $13.12 per share. [Yahoo Finance]

In a November 6 article on the earnings call, Yahoo Finance reported that LifeStance raised its full-year 2026 outlook. The article also said the company stated that its planned electronic health record transition could temper revenue growth in 2027. [Yahoo Finance]
