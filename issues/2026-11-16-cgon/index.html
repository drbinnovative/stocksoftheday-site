CG Oncology jumps after Phase 3 bladder cancer trial succeeds

The pick

CG Oncology (CGON) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on November 16, 2026. Volume was 9.0x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 13.95%.

What happened

On November 16, 2026, CG Oncology announced positive topline results from PIVOT-006, a Phase 3 registrational trial. The trial tested cretostimogene grenadenorepvec in patients with intermediate-risk and high-grade Ta non-muscle invasive bladder cancer after complete tumor resection. The company said the trial met its primary endpoint.

Cretostimogene reduced the risk of recurrence by 68% compared with the control arm. The hazard ratio was 0.32 (95% CI: 0.23, 0.45; p<0.0001). Median follow-up was 17 months as of October 26, 2026. The company said there were no Grade 3 or greater treatment-related adverse events and no treatment-related discontinuations.

The trial enrolled 367 patients across about 90 sites in the United States and Canada. It compared cretostimogene with surveillance. The company said there are currently no U.S. FDA approved adjuvant treatment options for this group of about 50,000 patients.

The company also said that, based on FDA feedback, its first BLA submission in BCG-unresponsive bladder cancer will be broadened to include high-grade papillary-only disease. It now expects to complete that submission before the end of March 2027. The company cautioned that topline data come from a preliminary analysis and may change after a fuller review.
