argenx (ARGX) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on October 8, 2026. Volume was 9.3x its 30-day average, and the stock fell 11.83%.

What happened

On October 8, argenx announced positive topline results from a Phase 2 study of FB102 in adults with celiac disease. The company said the study met its primary endpoint, with a p-value of 0.0176.

The study enrolled 126 patients. argenx said the safety profile was consistent with prior studies, with no new safety signals. The company plans to advance FB102 into Phase 3 development.

The same morning, argenx also issued a press release titled "argenx Provides Update on Phase 3 UNITY Study of Efgartigimod SC in Sjögren's Disease."
