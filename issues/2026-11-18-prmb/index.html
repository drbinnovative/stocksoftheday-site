Primo Brands trading volume jumps as stock falls 5.32%

The pick

Primo Brands (PRMB) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on November 18, at 4.5x its 30-day average. The stock fell 5.32%.

What happened

On November 16, Primo Brands filed two reports with the SEC about a debt offering. Two of its indirect subsidiaries, Primo Water Holdings and Triton Water Holdings, began offering $700 million of senior unsecured notes.

Later that day, the company said the subsidiaries had priced the offering as $700 million of 7.625% Senior Notes due 2034 in a private placement. The company expects the offering to close on November 30, 2026, subject to customary conditions.

The company said the two subsidiaries expect to use the proceeds, along with cash on hand, to redeem their existing 6.250% Senior Notes due 2029. Those notes total $712.8 million in outstanding principal. The proceeds would also be used to redeem a separate $0.2 million of 6.250% Senior Notes due 2029 issued by Triton Water Holdings, and to cover related fees and expenses.

The redemption price is $1,000.00 per $1,000.00 of principal, plus any accrued and unpaid interest. The redemptions depend on the offering closing.

The filings say nothing about the stock price. We couldn't find a clear reason for the move in the company's filings.
