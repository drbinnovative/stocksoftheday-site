What happened

HealthSpan Partners, an affiliate of Bon Secours Mercy Health, announced a definitive agreement to acquire Surgery Partners in an all-cash deal. The deal has an enterprise value of approximately $5.7 billion. Shareholders other than Bain Capital affiliates will receive $23.50 in cash per share.

Bain Capital affiliates will roll over their shares into equity interests in the acquiring entity. Bain is the largest shareholder, with approximately 39% of the outstanding shares. It agreed to vote all of its shares in favor of the deal.

A special committee of independent directors led the negotiations. After its unanimous recommendation, the Surgery Partners board approved the deal. HealthSpan Partners plans to pay with cash on hand and new debt, and says financing commitments have been secured.

After closing, Surgery Partners' common stock will no longer be listed on Nasdaq, and the company will become privately held.
