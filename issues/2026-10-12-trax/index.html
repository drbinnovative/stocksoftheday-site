The pick

First Tracks Biotherapeutics (TRAX) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on October 12. Volume was 9.8x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 6.91%.

What happened

On October 12, First Tracks announced positive top-line results from a gluten-challenge study of ANB033 in celiac disease. ANB033 is an investigational CD122 antagonist. The company said it had a statistically significant and clinically meaningful protective effect on both gluten-induced intestinal damage and symptoms.

The company reported two measures of intestinal damage. The mean change from baseline in the Vh:Cd ratio was -0.17 on ANB033 versus -0.66 on placebo, a +0.49 placebo-adjusted difference (p=0.009). The mean change in IEL density was -1.20 on ANB033 versus +18.83 on placebo, a -20.03 placebo-adjusted difference (p<0.001).

The company also said ANB033 prevented an increase in symptoms throughout the entire gluten challenge.

First Tracks filed an 8-K on October 13 that included a press release as an exhibit.
