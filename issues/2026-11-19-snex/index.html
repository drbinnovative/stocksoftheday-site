StoneX Shares Fall After Fiscal Fourth-Quarter Earnings Report

The pick

StoneX Group (SNEX) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on November 19. Volume was 5.3x its 30-day average, and the stock fell 26.71%.

What happened

On November 18, 2026, StoneX filed an 8-K saying it had issued a press release on its results of operations and financial condition. The release covered the fiscal quarter and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.

The filing text we have does not include the results themselves or explain the price drop.

Source: [SEC filing]

What news reports said

Yahoo Finance, carrying the company's earnings release, reported that StoneX had record fiscal year net income of $525.4 million, up 72%. It also reported quarterly net operating revenues of $655.2 million, up 12%, quarterly net income of $84.2 million and quarterly diluted EPS of $0.66 per share [Yahoo Finance].

Investing.com, in a piece carried by Yahoo Finance, reported that fourth-quarter diluted EPS of $0.66 fell short of the consensus estimate of $0.81. It also reported that the stock declined 6.2% in after-hours trading on Wednesday after the announcement. [Yahoo Finance]

Yahoo Finance reported that growth in the commercial and institutional businesses helped offset a weaker fourth quarter for securities trading and self-directed retail. The Motley Fool's earnings call transcript was summarized as "Record results were offset by a sharp retail slowdown and investment losses". [Yahoo Finance] [The Motley Fool]

Benzinga published an article titled "Why Are StoneX Shares Dropping on Thursday?". [Benzinga]
