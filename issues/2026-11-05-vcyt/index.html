Veracyte Stock Drops 54.30% the Day After Earnings Report

The pick

Veracyte (VCYT) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level. About 11.4x its 30-day average changed hands. The stock fell 54.30%.

What happened

On November 4, 2026, Veracyte issued a press release with its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The company also filed its quarterly report (Form 10-Q) on November 5.

In the same 8-K filing, Veracyte said it had started a share repurchase program. The program lets it buy back up to $100.0 million of its common stock over one year. The company said it is not required to buy any set number of shares and can change or stop the program at any time.

The 8-K does not state a reason for the stock's move.

Sources: [8-K filing] [10-Q filing]

What news reports said

Yahoo Finance reported that Veracyte lowered its near-term expectations for its Decipher Prostate test after volume came in below management's forecast [Yahoo Finance].

Another Yahoo Finance article reported that Veracyte posted $151.8 million in third-quarter revenue and authorized a $100 million buyback. It also reported that the company reset its 2027 growth expectations amid competitive pressure and heavier commercial investment. Yahoo Finance also reported that revenue grew 15% in the quarter.
