Huron Consulting trades 8.1x normal volume before S&P index move

The pick

Huron Consulting Group (HURN) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on November 2. Volume was 8.1x its 30-day average, and the price rose 0.29%.

What happened

Huron is set to join the S&P SmallCap 600 index. It will replace Supernus Pharmaceuticals (SUPN) before the opening of trading on Tuesday, November 3.

The change was originally set for Monday, November 2, the day of this volume spike. S&P Dow Jones Indices moved the date because of a special dividend ex-date announcement tied to Indivior Pharmaceuticals' acquisition of Supernus. S&P Dow Jones Indices had announced the original November 2 date in an October 22, 2026 press release.

The press release does not say how the change affected trading in Huron's shares.

Source: [press release]
