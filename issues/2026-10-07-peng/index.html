What happened

On October 6, 2026, Penguin Solutions reported fourth-quarter and full-year fiscal 2026 results for the period ended August 28, 2026. The company called the results records across key financial metrics. Net sales were a record $567 million, up 68% year over year. Net income was a record $93 million, up 888%. Diluted EPS was $1.29, compared with $0.11 a year earlier. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.00, compared with $0.43.

The company also raised its outlook for fiscal 2027. It now expects net sales of approximately $2.43 billion at the midpoint, which is growth of approximately 40%, plus or minus 10 percentage points. Last quarter's preliminary view implied $2.17 billion at the midpoint. The company also expects non-GAAP diluted EPS of $4.45.

Penguin said it won six new AI Infrastructure data center customers in the quarter, including four neocloud providers. Separately, it listed a win with a neocloud customer that has $10 billion in contracted compute from a leading AI lab. That customer chose Penguin to deploy and operate a 36,000-GPU AI factory in Norway.

The same day, the company named Stephen Cumming as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective immediately. Aaron Johnson, who had been Interim CFO since July 2026, returns to his role as Vice President of Finance and Accounting.
