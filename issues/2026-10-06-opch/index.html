What happened

On October 6, 2026, Option Care Health, CD&R and McKesson announced a definitive agreement. CD&R and McKesson will acquire Option Care Health for $32.05 per share. The deal reflects a total enterprise value of about $5.8 billion.

The price is a premium of about 37% to the October 5, 2026 closing share price. CD&R will hold a majority interest of about 51%. McKesson will invest about $1.4 billion for a minority interest of about 49%.

The deal is expected to close in the first half of calendar year 2027. It is subject to customary closing conditions, including approval by Option Care Health's stockholders and required regulatory approvals. After closing, the stock will no longer be listed on Nasdaq, and the company will become privately held.

Option Care Health's board unanimously concluded the transaction maximizes value for stockholders. The company also withdrew its previously disclosed financial guidance.
