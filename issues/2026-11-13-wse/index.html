Wise Group trades 8.2x its usual volume after half-year results

The pick

Wise Group plc (WSE) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level. Volume was 8.2x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 2.74%.

What happened

On November 13, Wise filed its results for the six months ended September 30, 2026. Active customers rose 21% to 16.2 million. Cross-border volume was $141.0 billion, up 24%. Net revenue was $1,453.1 million, up 23%.

Income before tax rose 9% to $334.9 million. Net income rose 6% to $249.2 million. Total operating expenses rose 28% to $1,150.3 million.

As of November 12, Wise said it expects fiscal 2027 net revenue growth in the upper half (17.5-20%) of its 15-20% medium-term target range, on a constant currency basis.

The filing also said Wise's Belgian subsidiary had been engaging with the Brussels prosecutor's office about queries on its business. It said an agreement has now been reached to fully resolve the matter, with no admission of wrongdoing.

Wise began a new share repurchase program in July 2026, expected to be over $500 million. It said it has bought almost 50% of the expected total. In a November 10 filing, Wise reported buying 476,311 shares on Nasdaq and 767,173 shares in London from November 2 to November 6.
