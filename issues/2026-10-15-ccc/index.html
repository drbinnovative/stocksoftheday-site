CCC Intelligent Solutions (CCC) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on October 15, 2026. Volume was 25.2x its 30-day average, while the price changed -0.13%.

What happened

CCC announced it has signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by a group of private investment firms led by GTCR, including Elliott Investment Management. The deal is all cash and has an enterprise value of approximately $6.1 billion.

CCC stockholders will receive $7.75 in cash for each share. The company said this is a premium of approximately 44% to its unaffected closing price on July 9, 2026, the last trading day before media speculation about a possible sale.

The deal was approved by CCC's Board of Directors. It is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions. These include required regulatory approvals and approval by CCC stockholders. The deal has no financing condition.

Until the deal closes, CCC stays publicly traded and keeps operating as an independent company. After it closes, CCC will be privately held and its stock will no longer be listed on the Nasdaq.
