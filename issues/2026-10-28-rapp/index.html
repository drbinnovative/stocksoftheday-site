The pick

Rapport Therapeutics (RAPP) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level. Volume was 6.9x its 30-day average, and the stock fell 18.30%.

What happened

A Rapport press release dated Oct. 27, carried by Yahoo Finance, said the company had started separate underwritten public offerings of $200.0 million of common stock and $150.0 million aggregate principal amount of notes. It also mentioned a concurrent private placement [Yahoo Finance].

A second Rapport press release dated Oct. 28, also carried by Yahoo Finance, announced pricing. It said about $175.0 million of common stock was priced at $21.00 per share. The headline also mentions pre-funded warrants, convertible notes and a concurrent private placement. [Yahoo Finance]

What news reports said

  • Yahoo Finance, citing Investing.com, reported that Rapport shares fell 20% in premarket trading on Wednesday after the pricing of $350 million in combined stock and convertible note offerings [Yahoo Finance].
  • Benzinga reported in a headline that Rapport shares sank 20% after hours [Benzinga].
  • Yahoo Finance reported on Oct. 25 that Rapport had announced positive top-line Phase II results for RAP-219 in acute bipolar mania. It said the trial met its primary and secondary endpoints and supported plans for Phase III development [Yahoo Finance]. That report does not link the results to the Oct. 28 price move.
