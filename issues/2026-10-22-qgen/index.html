QIAGEN (QGEN) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on October 22, 2026. Volume was 15.2x its 30-day average, and the stock closed up 0.44%.

What happened

QIAGEN signed a business combination agreement dated 21 October 2026 with Crown NL Bidco B.V. The agreement says the buyer intends to acquire QIAGEN through a full public tender offer for all of its issued ordinary shares.

QIAGEN's shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange and on the Prime Standard of the Frankfurt Stock Exchange.

The agreement says QIAGEN's managing board and supervisory board unanimously determined that the offer and the other transactions are in the best interest of the company and its associated enterprise.

It also says EQT Partners GmbH, acting as investment advisor to EQT AB and its affiliates, signed a confidentiality agreement with QIAGEN on 20 August 2026.
