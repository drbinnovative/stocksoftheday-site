The pick

ADT Inc. (ADT) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level on October 29, 2026. Volume was 19.9x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 33.20%.

What happened

ADT announced a definitive agreement to be acquired by Thrive Holdings in an all-cash deal. ADT stockholders will receive $10.00 per share in cash. The agreement values ADT at about $7.6 billion in equity value and $15.3 billion in enterprise value.

The price is a 45.6% premium to ADT's unaffected closing share price of $6.87 on October 27, 2026. It is also a 52.1% premium to the 30-day volume-weighted average share price ending that day. ADT's board unanimously approved the agreement.

ADT said in an 8-K that it entered into a merger agreement on October 29, 2026. The deal needs approval from ADT's stockholders, expiration or early termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust waiting period, and other customary closing conditions. The company said the deal is not subject to a financing condition and expects it to close in the first quarter of 2027.

The agreement includes a "go-shop" period. ADT may seek competing proposals until 11:59 p.m. New York City time on November 28, 2026. Certain parties that submit proposals in that period may get a 10-day extension. When the deal closes, ADT will become privately held and its stock will stop trading on the New York Stock Exchange.
