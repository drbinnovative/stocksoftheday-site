The pick

Mattel (MAT) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level. Volume was 8.5x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 18.80%.

What happened

On September 30, 2026, Mattel's board announced that Roger Lynch will become Chairman effective October 2, 2026, and Chief Executive Officer effective on or before November 2, 2026. Lynch is a current board member and Independent Lead Director.

He succeeds Ynon Kreiz, who will step down as Chairman and CEO effective October 2, 2026, to take a senior leadership position at another public company. The board named Diana Ferguson as its new Independent Lead Director.

Mattel said the appointment followed a comprehensive succession planning process. The company's filing does not say anything about the stock's move on October 1.

What news reports said

Yahoo Finance, summarizing the Wall Street Journal report, said the Barbie maker had attracted a potential offer of more than $20 per share. A Yahoo Finance article reported that Mattel rose as much as 36 percent during the session on reports that Authentic Brands Group was targeting the company for $6 billion.
