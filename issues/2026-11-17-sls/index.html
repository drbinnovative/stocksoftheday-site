SELLAS Stock Jumps After Company Reports Phase 3 Trial Met Goal

The pick

SELLAS Life Sciences (SLS) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level, at 16.9x its 30-day average. The stock rose 156.36%.

What happened

SELLAS announced topline results from its Phase 3 REGAL trial of galinpepimut-S (GPS), a cancer vaccine for acute myeloid leukemia (AML). The company said the trial met its primary endpoint of overall survival.

The company reported a 41% reduction in the risk of death for GPS, with a hazard ratio of 0.59 and a two-sided p-value of 0.035. Median overall survival was 24.0 months for patients on GPS and 14.7 months for those on best available treatment. The trial randomized 126 patients 1:1 between the two groups.

SELLAS said GPS was generally well tolerated, with no new safety signals identified. It said most treatment-related side effects were mild to moderate, most commonly reactions at the injection site.

The company said it plans to meet with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to discuss an accelerated filing strategy. It also said it plans to advance preparations for a Biologics License Application (BLA) submission for GPS in the first half of 2027. Later on November 17, SELLAS announced an underwritten public offering of $150.0 million of its common stock. It intends to give the underwriters a 30-day option to buy up to an additional $22.5 million of shares.
