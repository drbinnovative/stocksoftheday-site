What news reports said

Investing.com reported that Innodata shares fell on Tuesday following reports that the AI services company laid off nearly 550 employees in India [Yahoo Finance]. Seeking Alpha reported that the stock was down in premarket trading after the same reports, with additional job cuts reported in the Philippines [Seeking Alpha].

Yahoo Finance reported that the termination notices reportedly pointed to a "ramp-down of the business assignment." It said this raises the question of whether customer work is shrinking. [Yahoo Finance]

The Motley Fool reported that the layoffs spooked investors who fear Innodata just lost a major AI client. That is the outlet's description, and the sources we found don't confirm that a client was lost. [The Motley Fool]
