Kymera Jumps 30% After Positive Phase 2b Eczema Drug Results

The pick

Kymera Therapeutics (KYMR) had the biggest jump in trading volume compared with its usual level. Volume was 8.4x its 30-day average, and the stock rose 30.40%.

What happened

On November 23, 2026, Kymera announced results from BROADEN2, a Phase 2b trial of KT-621. KT-621 is an oral, once-daily drug that breaks down a protein called STAT6. It was tested in adults and adolescents with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis, a type of eczema. The company said the trial met its primary and secondary endpoints, with statistically significant improvements versus placebo at Week 16 across all doses.

The trial enrolled 244 patients. In the mid dose group, skin disease scores (EASI) fell by a mean of 72%, versus 27% for placebo. The company reported that 62% of mid dose patients reached EASI-75 (at least a 75% improvement), versus 22% on placebo. It also said KT-621 was well tolerated with a favorable safety profile.

The company said KT-621's efficacy profile was at least comparable to approved biologics that act on the same pathway. It also noted that no head-to-head trials have been run against dupilumab, so cross-trial comparisons may not be reliable.

Kymera also announced the same day that it had started an underwritten public offering of $1.5 billion of its common stock. All of the shares are being offered by Kymera.
