Privacy Policy

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy details the policies and procedures of DRB Innovative Products LLC, doing business as Stocks of the Day (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), regarding the collection, use, retention, protection, and disclosure of personal information obtained from users of stocksoftheday.com (the “Website”), our daily email newsletters, alerts, feeds, and paid memberships (collectively, the “Services”).

By accessing the Website, subscribing to our publications, or otherwise utilizing the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. This Policy applies strictly to information collected through stocksoftheday.com, our email delivery infrastructure, and directly integrated billing workflows. It does not apply to third-party websites, external portals, or social media platforms that you may access through links provided within our Services.

2. Information We Collect Directly from You

We practice strict data minimization and collect only the necessary information required to deliver our automated publications and manage subscriber accounts.

When you subscribe to our publications or create an account, we collect:

  • Identity Data. Your first name, last name, and primary email address.
  • Subscriber Preferences. Survey responses and account choices, such as your investing topics, Pro early-access selection, or AI tool interests.
  • Communications. Any correspondence, direct support tickets, feedback messages, or inquiries sent to our support team to ensure effective communication and issue resolution.

3. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the personal data we collect strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Delivering daily email newsletters, market analysis, and subscription alerts;
  • Managing subscriber accounts, billing, and Pro access privileges;
  • Personalizing email selections based on your indicated preferences;
  • Distributing occasional partner communications, sponsored content, and affiliate offers (which are always clearly and explicitly labeled as such);
  • Processing customer support requests and resolving technical issues;
  • Maintaining platform security, preventing fraud, and enforcing our Terms of Service.

4. Financial Metadata and Payment Processing Information

If you purchase a Pro Subscription, your payment is processed securely through our trusted third-party merchant gateways, specifically Stripe and Beehiiv.

We do not process, collect, or store raw credit card numbers, CVV security codes, full bank account credentials, or sensitive financial authentication tokens on our own infrastructure. Instead, we receive and retain only limited billing metadata necessary for accounting and account verification, which includes tokenized transaction receipts, card brand identifiers, the last four digits of the payment card, expiration dates, and billing history.

5. Automated Telemetry and Usage Data

When you interact with our Website or open our daily emails, our systems automatically record technical telemetry and usage metrics to maintain site performance and security.

Our newsletter delivery platform tracks delivery confirmations, email open rates, specific link click-through metrics, and general engagement levels to monitor email deliverability and improve content relevance. Simultaneously, when you visit our Website, we utilize privacy-forward tools, specifically Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measure site traffic using cookie-free visitor counting without tracking individual visitors across external websites.

6. Our Policy Regarding Sensitive Personal Information

We strictly prohibit the collection or storage of sensitive personal information.

Subscribers and site visitors are explicitly instructed not to transmit, send, or upload unsolicited sensitive personal data such as Social Security numbers, driver’s license details, bank account credentials, tax identification numbers, biometric data, or health records to our support desks or contact forms. DRB Innovative Products LLC disclaims all liability for the accidental interception or exposure of sensitive data transmitted in violation of this restriction.

7. Third-Party Service Providers and Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties under any circumstances. We share subscriber data strictly with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform under contractual confidentiality and data security obligations.

Our core infrastructure sub-processors include:

  • Beehiiv for newsletter distribution, audience segmentation, and engagement analytics;
  • Stripe for PCI-DSS compliant credit card processing and recurring billing;
  • Cloudflare strictly for privacy-forward, cookie-free web analytics;
  • GitHub for static website code hosting and deployment workflows.

8. Legal Disclosures and Corporate Business Transfers

We may disclose your personal information if required to do so by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, property, safety, or the safety of our users and the public.

In the event of a corporate merger, acquisition, restructuring, asset sale, or business reorganization, subscriber data may be transferred to the successor entity, provided that the acquiring entity remains bound by the protective terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Retention Schedule and Storage Protocols

We retain your email address and active subscriber records for the duration of your active subscription or newsletter enrollment.

If you unsubscribe from our lists or cancel your Pro membership, your email address is placed on a secure suppression list to prevent future unwanted communications, and your personal profile data is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required for tax compliance, financial auditing, or fraud-prevention purposes.

10. User Rights and State Privacy Disclosures

Depending on your state or country of residence, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information.

California residents, pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of their personal information. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Furthermore, under California Civil Code Section 1798.83 (Shine the Light), we do not disclose personal information to external third parties for direct third-party marketing purposes. Non-U.S. subscribers subject to the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) may request data access, rectification, deletion, or restriction of processing by contacting us directly.

11. Global Privacy Control and Opt-Out Signals

We recognize universal browser-based opt-out preference signals, including the Global Privacy Control (GPC), where required by applicable state privacy statutes.

If your web browser or device broadcasts an authenticated GPC signal, our technical systems will process it as a valid request to opt out of any data sharing or targeted profiling workflows. Because we do not sell or share personal data for commercial advertising, our core operational posture remains fully aligned with these signals.

12. Automated Algorithmic Processing and Non-Profiling

The scanning tools and analytical outputs published on our Website are generated automatically based on market volume, pricing telemetry, and quantitative rules.

Our automated systems do not process, evaluate, or profile individual human behavior, consumer creditworthiness, or personal characteristics. We do not engage in automated individual decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning individual subscribers. All analytical emails and reports are distributed uniformly across our subscriber base.

13. Data Breach Incident Response Protocols

In the event of a confirmed security incident, cyberattack, or personal data breach compromising unencrypted user information, DRB Innovative Products LLC will execute internal incident response protocols.

We will assess the scope of the compromise and notify affected users and competent regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable statutory timelines and legal notification requirements.
