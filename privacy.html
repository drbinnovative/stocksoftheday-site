Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Stocks of the Day collects, how we use it and the choices you have. Stocks of the Day is operated by DRB Innovative Products LLC ("we," "us" or "our"). It covers stocksoftheday.com, our newsletter and any related emails and paid memberships (the "Service").

The short version: we collect your email address so we can send you the newsletter, plus basic information about how the newsletter and site are used. We don't sell your personal information, and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your email address when you subscribe, the optional topics you check (such as investing basics or making videos with AI), and anything else you choose to share, such as your name, survey answers or messages you send us.
  • Payment information. If you join Pro, your payment is handled by our payment processors (Beehiiv and Stripe). They receive your card details; we see only limited information, such as your name, the last four digits of your card and your billing history.
  • Email activity. Our newsletter platform records whether emails are delivered, opened and which links are clicked. This helps us understand what readers find useful and keep our sending reputation healthy.
  • Website activity. When you visit the site, our hosting and signup providers may collect standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you came from and which pages you visit. Our signup form also records how you found us (for example, a search engine or a social media post) so we can see which channels bring in readers.

2. How we use your information

  • To send you the newsletter and, if you're a member, Pro content and alerts.
  • If you check any optional topics, to send you occasional emails about those topics. These may include offers from partners and from other businesses our owner has an ownership stake in, and some links may earn us a commission. You can change your topics anytime using the "email preferences" link at the bottom of every email.
  • To manage memberships, process payments and send account emails like receipts and renewal reminders.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To understand how the Service is used and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

We don't use your information to give personalized investment advice. Everyone receives the same content.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service, under their own privacy and security commitments:

  • Beehiiv, our newsletter and membership platform, which stores subscriber lists and sends our emails.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • GitHub, which hosts our website.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts site visits without cookies and without tracking individual visitors.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business. In a sale, your information would remain subject to this policy, and we'd notify you.

Some issues may include advertisements or sponsored content. We don't give advertisers your personal information. We may share combined, non-identifying statistics, such as total subscriber counts or average open rates.

4. Cookies

Our site and signup form may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, measure visits and record how you arrived at the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features, like signup tracking, may not.

5. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Change your topics anytime using the "email preferences" link at the bottom of every email. Unchecking a topic stops emails about it without affecting the daily newsletter.
  • Access, correct or delete your information by emailing us. We'll respond within 30 days. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as payment records for tax purposes.
  • Cancel Pro anytime from your account settings, as described in our Terms of Service.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. Email us to exercise them; we won't treat you differently for doing so.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're subscribed. After you unsubscribe or close your membership, we delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period, except where we need to keep records for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Security

We rely on established providers with strong security practices, and we limit access to subscriber information. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has subscribed, contact us and we'll delete their information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll post the updated policy here and notify subscribers by email.
